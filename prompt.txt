I am building a healthcare technology platform called "CareWatch".

Project concept:
CareWatch is an AI-powered post-discharge monitoring and care-coordination platform. Its purpose is to detect possible patient deterioration after hospital discharge before the condition becomes severe.

The system should NOT act as an AI doctor. It should NOT diagnose diseases or prescribe medicines.

Instead, it should:
1. Create a personalized baseline when a patient is discharged.
2. Collect low-burden post-discharge information from the patient.
3. Analyze symptoms, vitals, activity/behavioral changes and historical trends.
4. Detect possible deterioration relative to the patient's own baseline.
5. Classify the situation into Low / Medium / High risk.
6. For medium-risk cases, request physical verification from an appropriate healthcare worker.
7. For high-risk or verified concerning cases, route the case to a doctor/clinical team.
8. Allow the doctor to make the final clinical decision.
9. Record the outcome and continue monitoring.

The key innovation is:
"Personalized longitudinal deterioration detection + accessible data collection + risk-adaptive physical verification + care coordination."

The system should work for both rural and urban patients.

2. PATIENT BASELINE
==================================================

When the patient is discharged, the system creates a personalized baseline.

Baseline should contain:

- Discharge vitals
- Relevant clinical parameters
- Symptoms at discharge
- Diagnosis
- Medication information
- Activity baseline if available
- Relevant lab values
- Monitoring schedule

Do NOT assume that one universal threshold applies to every patient.

The system should primarily analyze CHANGE FROM PERSONAL BASELINE plus clinically relevant thresholds.

Example:

Baseline SpO2 = 96
Current SpO2 = 92

Instead of simply saying:
"SpO2 is 92"

the system should recognize:

"SpO2 has decreased by 4 percentage points from the patient's discharge baseline."

7. RISK MODEL
==================================================

Initially implement a hybrid approach:

Clinical safety rules
+
ML risk model
+
Longitudinal trend analysis

Risk levels:

LOW:
No concerning change detected.

Action:
Continue automated monitoring.

MEDIUM:
Potential deterioration detected but physical verification is appropriate.

Action:
Create a field-health-worker task.

HIGH:
Strong concern or predefined emergency criteria.

Action:
Clinical review / predefined emergency escalation.

IMPORTANT:
Risk classification is NOT a medical diagnosis.
